# Unfurl

Unfurl's language for authoring and editing branching narratives.

## Language

**Node**:
A named unit of a branching story containing narrative content, metadata, and
outgoing choices.

**Metadata**:
Named properties attached to a story node that supplement its narrative content
and choices with information for game systems.

**Metadata definition**:
A project-level description of a metadata field and the kind of value it accepts.

**Narrative designer**:
A person who authors a story's content and branching structure.

**Technical collaborator**:
A person who inspects and corrects an existing story's data to resolve problems
in how the story interacts with game systems.
